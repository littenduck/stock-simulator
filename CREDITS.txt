## Market data (`data/market/history.csv`)
Daily open/high/low/close prices for 30 stocks (2022-2026), downloaded from Yahoo Finance with
`tools/fetch_history.py`. Fetched for personal, non-commercial prototyping. **The values in the CSV are modified: every price was multiplied by 0.1 (scaled down to one tenth) by `tools/fetch_history.py`, so they are processed data derived from Yahoo Finance, not the original quotes. Daily percentage moves are unchanged.** Scaling does not change where the data comes from, so the licensing caveat below still applies. Yahoo's terms may restrict
redistribution: check them (or switch to a licensed data source) before a public or commercial release.

## Audio (`audio/`)
Four sound packs by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), downloaded from kenney.nl, licence CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each is kept in its own folder under its original name,
with its original `License.txt`. Credit is not required but appreciated: "Sound effects by Kenney (kenney.nl)".

| Folder | Pack | Used by the game |
|---|---|---|
| `audio/kenney_interface-sounds/` | Interface Sounds 1.0 | yes, all effects (`ui/audio/audio_feedback.gd`) |
| `audio/kenney_ui-audio/` | UI Audio | yes (ticks, clicks) |
| `audio/kenney_digital-audio/` | Digital Audio | yes (skills, banners, fanfares) |
| `audio/kenney_impact-sounds/` | Impact Sounds 1.0 | yes (boards, stamps, punches) |

## Art / fonts
`icon.svg` belongs to the project. The company and perk icons are 16x16 pixel bitmaps written as text in
`ui/theme/pixel_icons.gd` (original work). The UI uses Godot's built-in default font; no font files are shipped.
UI panel/button textures: Kenney pixel UI pack in `ui/theme/assets/kenney_pixel-ui-pack/` (see the licence file in that folder).


## Music (`audio/music/`)
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `startmenu.ogg` | Start-screen theme, generated with Suno (a chiptune track from the project owner's own prompts) |
| `gameplay.ogg` | In-game background loop, generated with Suno from the owner's 4-bar theme; cut at a bar line so it loops seamlessly |
| `startmenu.mid`, `gameplay.mid` | Raw NES-style (pulse + triangle) versions of the two pieces as MIDI. Not used by the game (Godot does not import them); made with `tools/audio/render_game_8bit.py`, no samples |

The Suno tracks are subject to the terms of the Suno plan they were generated under: check them before a commercial release.
The Suno masters are not in the repository (about 25 MB as WAV); only the compressed .ogg files are.


## Character portraits (`ui/theme/portraits/`)
Mini Characters 2.0 by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters), licence CC0 1.0, copy in
`ui/theme/portraits/License.txt`. The six PNGs are renders of pack models: character-male-a (recoloured to the light skin tone of the other
characters), character-male-b (head painted goldfish orange, with a few red block fins added), character-robot,
character-male-e, character-male-d and character-female-e (the last three unchanged).
`tools/kenney_portraits.gd` makes the images and shows how to regenerate them from the downloaded pack. The six 3D models used by the opening animation are in `ui/theme/models/` (with the pack's `License.txt`). Credit is not required but appreciated:
"Characters by Kenney (kenney.nl)".

Font: Fusion Pixel Font 12px (proportional, zh_hans) by TakWolf (https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), licence SIL
Open Font License 1.1, files in `ui/theme/fonts/` (`OFL.txt`; the font merges glyphs from Ark Pixel (OFL), Cubic 11 (M+ BITMAP
FONTS, free to use and distribute) and Galmuri (OFL), whose licences are in `ui/theme/fonts/LICENSES/`). The font has no
Reserved Font Name and is used unmodified. The OFL does not ask for credit inside the game, only that the copyright notice
and the licence text travel with the font files; the font may not be sold on its own.
